# premium-soft — soft karaoke words + huge hero phrases half behind the head, light-led motion (DEFAULT grammar)
best for: personal brand, authority, any talking head (default)
kind: grammar · learned: built-in · learn focus: whole edit
source: built-in (craft/09 §9)

## Look
palette: footage + one accent (gradient accent → deep accent → plum on emphasis) · text #F2F0E8
type: karaoke word IBM Plex Sans Arabic 500 ~66 px · hero = light lead (Plex Light or Aref Ruqaa) ~74 px + Alexandria 900 118–135 px keyword with kashida
layout: hero phrase 30–50 % behind the head (matte), second bold line in front of the chest; hero blocks on the RIGHT · graphics are objects in the scene (lit sign on the wall, logo on the palm, orbiting cards, floating photo cards radius 22)

## Pacing
shots ~4.3 s · gimbal drift or a fake push 1.00→1.05 per shot · park/punch ladder

## Captions
karaoke word 1–2 at a time on the chest band, instant swap at word start −1 f (closest CAP-02) · hero replaces the karaoke word, never both

## Transitions
cuts 1–7 f: white flash (×3.2 brightness 2 f), whip (120 px blur 4 f), defocus jump (blur 18 over 4 f)

## Sound
whoosh 0.2–0.6 s before + sub boom ±1 f on the cut; SFX on almost every graphic event; music ~12 dB under the voice with ~2.5 dB ducking

- Never slant Arabic > 4°, never split letters; hero blocks mirrored to the right.

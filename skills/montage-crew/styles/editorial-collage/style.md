# editorial-collage — grayscale cut-out objects, paper texture, one colour disc, a phrase every 1–2 s
kind: grammar · source: built-in (craft/09 §7) · learned: built-in · best for: hooks, intros, manifesto lines, idea-only videos · learn focus: whole edit

## Look
palette: paper off-white or dark paper · grayscale cut-outs · ONE big colour disc in the accent
type: Alexandria 900 + Amiri/Aref Ruqaa contrast · crop marks, tape, torn edges
layout: one metaphor object (grayscale cut-out) + one phrase per beat, centred; disc behind the object

## Pacing
a new phrase every 1–2 s · object swaps on the beat (music or voice)

## Captions
the phrases ARE the captions (CAP-25 One Word or CAP-20 Editorial Serif)

## Motion graphics
| what | enter | hold | exit | SFX | id |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| cut-out object | stop-motion 2–3 steps (on 2s) | beat | hard cut | paper rustle | MG collage |
| colour disc | scale 0→1 back.out 1.6 8 f | beat | — | pop | MG shape |
| phrase | clip-wipe RTL 6–8 f | beat | hard cut | — | wipeRTL |

## Transitions
hard cuts on beats; paper-tear as the ONE hero transition

## Sound
music-led, paper/foley SFX, `beat.py` beat map

## How to use it
- Objects come from free-for-commercial sources (asset-sourcing.md), background removed, desaturated.
- Short: best ≤ 30 s or as the hook section of a longer video.

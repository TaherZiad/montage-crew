# chapter-looks — one look per sentence, loud transitions between looks, calm inside
kind: grammar · source: built-in (craft/09 §7) · learned: built-in · best for: short cinematic 20–40 s, mood pieces, travel, launches · learn focus: whole edit

## Look
palette: changes per chapter (each chapter = grade + type change), still one accent inside a chapter
type: clean text, one statement per chapter (Alexandria / Amiri / Aref Ruqaa by mood)
layout: centred statement, lots of air

## Pacing
one chapter per sentence (3–8 s), calm and static inside

## Captions
the statement is the caption (CAP-20 / CAP-18 Cinematic Lower)

## Motion graphics
| what | enter | hold | exit | SFX | id |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| chapter statement | fade + blur 9 f | chapter | dies on the transition | — | T-GLOW-TITLE (no glow) |
| strobe between looks | 13 f W1/A1/W1/B1/W1 | — | — | hit | X-FLASH/LEAK |
| film burn / glitch (pick one family) | 6–10 f | — | — | burn / glitch SFX | transitions-library |

## Transitions
loud and designed between chapters, none inside

## Sound
music-led, hit on each chapter change, voice dry

## How to use it
- Grading only here (the grammar needs it); keep skin natural.
- No strobe for viewers sensitive to flashing: keep flashes ≤ 3 per second.

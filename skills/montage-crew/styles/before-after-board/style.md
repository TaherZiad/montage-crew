# before-after-board — portfolio wrapper: small Before + big After, tilted editor timeline
best for: showcasing an edit, design or product result (portfolio posts)
kind: look · source: built-in (style-dna D/E/F/H boards) · learned: built-in · learn focus: wrapper layout

## Look
palette: grey satin board or dark board · text white · accent: one (blue gradient or brand)
type: two-line captions, white lead + gradient keyword (kashida)
layout: small BEFORE card + 1.5× AFTER card playing in sync; an editor timeline screenshot tilted below with a linear playhead · rotoscoped speaker optional with HUD/hex grid behind

## Pacing
18–30 s · whole-line 2 f pops · a flash/leak on every source change (measured)

## Captions
closest CAP-16 Two-Tone Lead (no pill by default; the reference's pill is CAP-07 ⚠ only if the user picks it)

## Motion graphics
| what | enter | hold | exit | SFX | id |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before/After cards | slide in 8 f, After scales 1.5× | whole video | — | whoosh | C-3D-EDGE-CARD |
| editor timeline + playhead | tilt 12°, playhead linear (the ONE linear motion allowed) | whole video | — | — | MG timeline |
| source-change flash/leak | 3–6 f | — | — | hit | X-FLASH/LEAK |
| CTA pill | 6 f | 1.5 s | fade 12 f | pop | O-CTA-FOLLOW |

## Transitions
flash/leak family (white glitch wipe, cyan bloom, film burn) — pick ONE family

## Sound
hit on every source change, music-led

## How to use it
- Wrap the user's own before/after material; never another creator's work.
- Works with no footage of the speaker at all.

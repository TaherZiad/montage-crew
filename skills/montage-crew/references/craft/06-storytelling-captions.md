# Editorial storytelling & caption design

Research: 22 transcripts + 6 contact sheets + 7 web. Paddy Galloway ×2, HillierSmith (MrBeast roast), Isaac, Kallaway, Logan Smith, Film Booth, Cutting Concepts (Murch Rule of Six), Creative Suite (CapCut), Patrik Key, Premiere Gal, Matt Loui, ryancollinsvideo (Hormozi captions), Jenny Hoyos interview (4.9M), Every Frame a Painting (2.8M), James Jani breakdown, Johnny Harris breakdown, Joe Sbiti, heyDominik (1000 hooks), Think Media/MrBeast, Justin Odisho, Kyle Hamrick; MrBeast production doc summaries, safe-zone guides, Netflix Timed Text guide, caption-trend articles. `[synth]` = our default.

## Editorial workflow (raw transcript → cut)
0. **Promise**: one-line premise/title before cutting; judge every keep/cut against it.
1. **Log**: tag each sentence HOOK-candidate / CONTEXT / STAKES / POINT / PROOF / STORY / EMOTION / PAYOFF / CTA / FILLER / REPEAT / TANGENT / FLUB; score novelty, emotion, clarity, quotability 0–3 `[synth]`.
2. **Selects** — remove: filler (يعني، اممم، فـ، "basically"), false starts (keep the last clean take), throat-clearing intros (e.g. «أهلاً جميعاً، اليوم سوف…» — adapt to the profile's dialect), hedges, duplicate points (keep the tighter), tangents unless funny/character/setup. Pauses 3–6 f between sentences; keep 12–30 f after emotional lines/reveals. Kill dead end-tails (Jenny Hoyos: −1 s ending → retention 83→88 %).
3. **Paper edit**: one kept sentence per line, grouped into 3–8 "progressions"; for each: why here? later? can a moment move earlier?
4. **Restructure**: strongest/most visual/surprising line → 0–3 s (even if said at minute 4); chain beats with **"but / therefore"**, never "and then"; value within ~4 s (shorts); compress early chronology; hold the payoff to the end; optional cold open on the climax then rewind.
5. **Hook sentence scoring**: topic clear in 1–2 s · contains/pairs with tension ("بس", "المشكلة", "except") · implies a payoff the video delivers · 3–5 words work on screen · works sound-off · grade-5 reading level · ≤ 12 words / ≤ 3 s including foreshadow. Template: **Context → "بس/but" → contrarian snapback / foreshadow**. A two-sentence Frankenbite only if meaning is preserved.
6. **Re-hooks**: sentences that open a question («الغريب أنّ…»، «ما لم أتوقعه…»، «هنا حدث الخطأ» — adapt to the profile's dialect) at segment boundaries; every 8–15 s in shorts; open the next loop in the same breath as closing one.
7. **Tension/payoff**: every setup has room to land and a later payoff; setup → why it matters → answer; escalate context→react ladders; ticking clock for long pieces.
8. **Rhythm**: shots/lines shrink toward climax, longer holds after (~1:2); cut on the eyes/decisions; Murch: emotion > story > rhythm > eye-trace > 2D plane > 3D continuity. Visual change every 2–4 s in the hook, 4–8 s later (long form).
9. **Distraction test**: watch full length, distracted; mark every urge to leave; fix.

## Emphasis system
Keyword priority: 1 numbers/money · 2 contrast/surprise words after "بس/but" · 3 recognisable names · 4 promise words (مجاني، سر، ممنوع) · 5 emotion verbs/adjectives · 6 topic noun in first 2 s. Never function words. Max one highlight per caption chunk.
| Layer | Shorts 9:16 | Long form |
|---|---|---|
| Base captions | always (70–85 % watch muted) | off or key lines only |
| Keyword highlight | 1 per chunk, ~2–3 / 10 s | — |
| Big text (2–5 words, ≥ 2× caption) | hook + ≤ 1 / 10–15 s | ~1 / 20–40 s |
| Emoji | ≤ 1 / 5–10 s, only if it adds meaning | rare |
Pairing: if the visual already shows the object, text names the meaning; big numbers behind/near the head; text in the scene; text match-cut (same word across documents); scale + colour + SFX on the spoken frame; never cover eyes/mouth; keep the focal point in the same place across cuts.

## Caption spec (1080×1920 @30, 9:16)
16:9 (1920×1080): keep text inside x 96–1824, y 54–1026; nothing important in the bottom 140 px (player controls).

| Param | Spec |

## Idea bank
context→but→snapback hook · hook + foreshadow in 2 lines ≤ 3 s · cold open on climax, then rewind · write/choose the last line first · crazy progression early · re-engagement beats (3 min / 6 min long form) · re-hook each point, delay the answer · open a new loop as one closes · but/therefore chaining · escalating reaction ladder · second voice restates the rule · keep one organic flub · twist on the promised payoff · countdown text ("١٠ أشهر باقية") · progress motif (checklist/counter) · let emotions breathe 0.5–1.5 s · tension ramp (shorter shots → longer hold) · pacing break without breaking pace (new location/angle/music) · music changes per section, drops before payoff · 3–5 word title text in hook naming the meaning · keyword colour pop only on numbers/contrasts/names · giant number behind subject with count-up · small connector word next to big keyword stacked (Matt Loui) · text match-cut across documents · Ken Burns 2.0 on stills (7–8 f settle, blur 30→0, projector tick) · text in the scene · plain white text on black as a punchline card · arrow/circle + 1–3 words on the detail · emoji only as visual pun · end on the payoff reaction, no "so that's it".

## Mistakes
cutting so fast setups don't register (Paddy's #1 note) · copying "fast cuts" instead of clarity/stakes · weakest segment first because it was filmed first · hook without context · payoff that disappoints / mismatches hook · dwelling after a storyline ends · captions too big / in UI zones / over mouth · captions on every word in long form, several accent colours · uncorrected transcript typos · highlight out of sync · preset animation overload · rushing emotions · loud SFX · "that's it guys" + dead tail.

## Editorial QA
1 promise stated/visible 0–3 s, hook works sound-off · 2 hook = context + contrast + implied payoff, ≤ 12 words · 3 value ≤ 4 s · 4 no filler/false starts/duplicates; pauses ≤ 6 f except intentional · 5 re-hook/new loop at every boundary · 6 every setup paid off; no answer before its "why" · 7 but/therefore, no "and then" > 20 s · 8 biggest emotions get 12–30 f · 9 visual change every 2–4 s in hook; no static > 8–10 s in shorts · 10 captions ≤ 20 chars/line, ≤ 2 lines, ≤ 20 chars/s, ≥ 0.8 s, inside 900×1400, off eyes/mouth · 11 one accent, ≤ 1 highlight per chunk · 12 transcript proofread, sync ±1 f · 13 contrast ≥ 4.5:1 · 14 Arabic: correct shaping, word-level animation only, punctuation mirrored · 15 ends on payoff, no tail, loop point · 16 distraction test done · 17 on-screen text ≤ grade-5 reading level.

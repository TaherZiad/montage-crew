## Catalog (frames @30)
1. **Smooth velocity zoom** — hero transition for talking heads. 16–30 f (15+15). A `expo.in` 1→2.5(–3) (+rotate ±3–8° same sign both sides), cut at peak, B `expo.out` 0.25–0.6→1; radial blur 0→~25 px at cut →0. Edge fill for B < 1×: 3×3 mirror tile or blurred copy at 1.3. Real motion blur: render 120 fps → `tmix=frames=4,fps=30`; cheap: 3 ghost copies ×1.02/1.04/1.06 at .5/.3/.15 over peak 6 f. SFX whoosh peak on cut (start 6–8 f early), sub-boom on drops.
   ```js
   tl.to(A,{scale:2.5,rotation:4,duration:15/30,ease:"expo.in"},t0).set(A,{autoAlpha:0},t0+15/30)
     .fromTo(B,{scale:.4,rotation:-4},{scale:1,rotation:0,duration:15/30,ease:"expo.out"},t0+15/30);
   ```

## Rules
- **Frequency**: talking-head ≈ 95 % hard cuts + punch-ins; stylised transitions only at section boundaries, never mid-sentence. Shorts: visual change every 1.5–3 s, but a real stylised transition at most every 8–15 s — **one hero transition per short** (hook→body or drop). Montage: every downbeat OK only within one family.
- **One family per video** (whip + zoom + flash, OR light leak + blur + film burn). Never mix glitch + film burn + 3D cube.
- **Direction continuity**: A's exit = B's entry direction; zoom-in continues as zoom-in; rotation keeps its sign. Arabic content: "forward" may read right→left — stay consistent.
- **Velocity matching**: peak speed exactly on the cut frame (ease-in out of A, ease-out into B); equal peak speed and blur both sides; motion blur for moves > 1/3 frame width per frame.
- **Timing**: cut between sentences/breaths; flash/cut/glitch transient on kick/snare; whoosh starts ~150–250 ms early; subliminal flashes ≤ 2 f; ≤ 3 flashes per second (photosensitivity).
- **Cheap tells**: star/heart/page-curl/cube presets; ripple dissolve/iris; long dissolves in fast videos; glitch everywhere or on emotional content; watermarked/low-res overlays; no motion blur / linear easing; mixed directions; transitions covering the speaking face; unmotivated 3D spins; a transition on every cut; same preset > 3×.
- **Don't transition** on serious/sad/medical topics, testimonials, interviews (use J/L), continuous action already cut on motion, the first 1–2 s of the hook, or wherever a cut works. Test: does it show time/place/idea change or hit a beat? Would removing it hurt? If not — delete.

## Mistakes / QA
Mistakes: black edges when scaling < 1× / sliding · SVG filter region clipping · non-deterministic randomness · easing peak not on the cut · isotropic blur for whips/zooms · zoompan jitter (upscale first) · xfade input mismatch (size/fps/pix_fmt/timebase; offset = lenA − d) · grain before scaling · overlays in normal blend (black boxes) · SFX 1–2 f late · captions caught in glitch/shake unintentionally.
QA per transition: 1 stated purpose · 2 on sentence boundary / beat · 3 direction & rotation continue · 4 peak velocity/blur on the cut, symmetric · 5 durations in spec (whip 8–10, glitch 6–10, zoom 16–30, flash ≤ 10) · 6 no black edges/clipping (frame-step) · 7 motion blur on fast moves · 8 SFX ±1 f; music ducks under freezes · 9 face visible while speaking, captions readable · 10 one family, no preset > 3× · 11 seeded — identical re-renders · 12 on a phone at 1×: if you notice the transition before the content, cut intensity/duration ~30 % · 13 ≤ 3 flashes/s.

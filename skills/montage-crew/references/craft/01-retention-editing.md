# Retention editing for short-form talking-head

Research: 24 YouTube tutorials (incl. 3 Arabic) + 7 web sources. Key sources: Learn By Leo (5.8M), Joseph|Video Editing (Hormozi/Ali Abdaal/Iman breakdowns), Kallaway, heyDominik "1000 hooks", Logan Smith, Joris Hermans, Isaac, Cutting Concepts, Nate Curtiss, Ryan Herrick, Tark, John Hague, Dylan Reynolds, Mostafa Salah (AR), Wagih Alnajjar (AR), MoneyLab University (AR); Shortzly, MrBeast production doc, Virvid (loops), safe-zone guides. `[inferred]` = our conversion.

## Principles (ranked by agreement)
1. **Hook decides everything; topic clear in 1–3 s** (14 sources). Drop-off cliff is in the first 2 s; viewers scroll on autopilot. Say what it's about, then open a gap.
2. **Kill dead air, keep rhythm** (13). Cut right before silence ends/speech starts, J-cuts. Counterweight: keep a deliberate pause before punchlines/emotional lines.
3. **Show, don't tell — visuals extend words, don't repeat them** (11). Literal stock that echoes words = amateur.
4. **Constant but motivated visual change** (11) — every change tied to meaning; unmotivated change = "visual mush".
5. **Captions mandatory: short, animated, keyword-emphasised, in safe zone** (10). Muted viewing; text = second pacing layer.
6. **Sound design is half the edit** (10) — every motion gets a sound; music changes per section; music drop on key line. Not too loud, not per caption.
7. **Don't over-edit; match density to audience/brand** (8). Hormozi shorts ≈ 90 % plain text.
8. **Hold the payoff to the end; keep loops open** (6) — Zeigarnik.
9. **Continuity of attention** (5): keep eye-line/focus point in the same screen spot across cuts; graphics have entry + exit.
10. **Consistent brand system** (5): 2 fonts, 3–4 colours (60-30-10), one caption style.
11. **Loop the ending into the opening** (replays → completion > 100 %).

## Parameters (30 fps)
**Hook 0–3 s**
- Topic in first spoken line within 1–2 s; "you/your", pain point, contrast A vs B in next 1–2 lines.
- Open on the strongest visual/result (Mostafa: show the finished dish first).
- Native-looking text hook: white rounded box + emoji at y 25–35 %, 30–60 f; suppress captions meanwhile.
- Motion in hook: zoom-out 115–120 %→100 % over 10–15 f, or snap punch-in on key word. Optional low riser (~−20 dB).
- Hook stays on the face (no B-roll) unless the visual IS the hook; allowed: rotoscoped effect behind speaker on the key word.

**Pacing**
- Visual change: TikTok every 1.5–3 s, Reels 2.5–4 s, Shorts 3–5 s. New idea every 3–7 s.
- Breath between phrases ≤ 2–4 f (high energy) `[inferred]`; keep 8–15 f pause before punchlines (max ~2 per short).
- J-cut: audio leads video 3–4 f.
- 2-cam: switch on new sentence/paragraph or hand gesture, never mid-word. Podcast: wide/tight every 1–2 s in hook, then 3–6 s.
- 1-cam: alternate 100 % / 110–115 % per segment, eye-line fixed.
- Pattern interrupt (B-roll, graphic, camera change, music drop) ≥ every 5–8 s.
- Big transitions only at section changes (1–2 per short). Overlays/transitions ≤ ~40 % of runtime.

**Zooms**
- Emphasis punch: snap to 115–130 % in 0–2 f on key word, return at next cut.
- Smooth push: 100→105–108 % across the shot.
- Paragraph-start zoom: 110→100 % over 6–10 f ease-out, then slow drift forward (Wagih, AR).
- No zoom in shots < ~1 s (nest shots, one zoom across them). Motion blur on fast zooms.

**Captions**
- 1–3 words, ≤ 10–15 chars/line, ≤ 2 s each; single words for emphasis.
- Centre at 50–65 % H (just under the chin); 9:16: never in top 220–250 px or bottom 400–480 px; side margins ≥ 60–90 px.
- Heavy sans; white + black shadow/glow, or black on white rounded box; optional black gradient on bottom 30 %.
- Keyword: ~1 per caption, +110–130 %, colour by meaning (green +, red −, yellow neutral), optional emoji.
- Animations: pop 70→110 % (4 f) → 100 % (3 f); scale-in 80→100 % ease-out; slide-up 20 f cubic-out; posterize 12–15 fps for handmade feel.
- No SFX on ordinary caption pops. Titles ≠ captions in size/weight. Text-behind-subject for key words.

**B-roll**: 3–5 s max each; rhythm B / 1–2 A / B; cut on action start; show consequence not literal noun; proof screenshots 0.5–1 s; spotlight (darken/blur surroundings, feathered ellipse); hue-shift red/green for bad/good; arrows/circles drawn with trim paths; framed B-roll card over blurred copy; ≤ 3 elements on screen.

**Sound**: music 5–25 dB under VO (or EQ-carve 1–4 kHz); whoosh ≈ −10 dB vs voice; SFX peak on fastest/blurriest frame; palette: whoosh (move), pop/click/ding (appear), typing (typewriter), cash register (money), shutter (photos), riser→hit (reveal), reverse hit (pre-lap), drone (tension); change track at topic shifts; hard-stop music 1–2× per short on the key line.

**Ending/loop**: payoff in last 10–20 %; last line flows into first line; no silent tail; visual match last↔first frame; best length 20–35 s; specific CTA ("follow for part 2").

**Export**: 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 20–40 Mbps, AAC 320 k.

## Arabic notes
- Proper shaping engine (HarfBuzz/libass/browser) — never trust CapCut/Premiere defaults; bidi breaks "20$", "ChatGPT" inside Arabic lines — test.
- Arabic reels style: yellow/white bold Arabic under the chin over dark gradient, 2–3 words; text behind subject; rotoscoped bg swaps; light-leak/film-burn flashes; sentence-exact fast cuts; zoom at each paragraph start.
- ~2 words per caption (Arabic words are wider), +10–20 % line-height, real Arabic weights (no faux bold/italic); emphasis by colour/scale (no caps); motion enters from the right / reveals RTL; "before" on the right, "after" on the left; karaoke highlight moves RTL.
- Captions follow the spoken dialect, not MSA.

# Style DNA — cross-reference synthesis (8 measured Arabic reels)

Eight agency-grade Arabic reels were measured frame by frame. They define the **quality bar and the technique vocabulary — never a template to copy** (no text, layout sequence, brand look or object is reused 1:1). Each is named here by its look.
Frames @30 fps unless noted. H = frame height, W = frame width (1080×1920). For 16:9 use the same ratios of H/W.

## The 12 laws — detail
1. **Keywords not subtitles** (when the coverage mode is keywords). 1 hero word (5–13 % H) + 1 subline (2.2–3 % H). Karaoke-style word stacks only as a deliberate beat (T-WORD-STACK) or on talk-only stretches (2-word chunks, 2.8 % H at y 46 %). With full phrase captions (the default coverage), hero words appear only on the key beats.
2. **Weights**: Alexandria 800/900 + 200/300, IBM Plex Sans Arabic 700 + 300, Cairo/Tajawal Black. Ratio 2.2–3:1.
3. **Kashida**: `head` fades/rises 2-f stagger (f0–6) → bar scaleX 0→1 from right (f4–16, quart.out) → tail lands (f14–22). Width target 1.5–2.5 em, or to justify to the widest line.
4. **Timing**: graphic enters 0–6 f after a cut / on the spoken word (±3 f). Subline +4–6 f. Hold until next cut. Captions/keywords change every 0.6–1.3 s in dense reels; hero beats hold 1–2.5 s.
5. **Palette**: dark crushed-shadow background (never pure black), skin kept natural. Text cream/ivory/white. One accent. Glow = 2 layers: tight (4–12 px, 80 %) + wide (30–60 px, 40–60 %).
6. **Zones**: titles y 3–18 %; hero torso 45–70 %; glass/cards 55–100 %; vertical text on edges 5–25 % W. Face (MED: top 35 % of frame around head) always clear.
7. **Depth**: blurred foreground blobs (blur 18–60 px, 60–90 % opacity, drift 2–4 %W/s), 3D cards cropped off corners behind speaker, numerals/ghost letters (8–12 % opacity) behind the matte.
8. **Camera**: cut punch-in 150–185 %; animated zoom-out 5–9 f expo.out; push-in 12–17 f inOut; in-shot push 1→1.06 linear. Anchor at face (50 %, 42–45 %). Default for the skill: smooth eased zooms (see craft/12).
9. **Motion**: in 5–15 f; pops `back.out(1.4–1.7)`; slides `expo.out`/`quart.out`; exits 3–8 f blur-fade or reverse build (un-type, bars→pill→dot). Push old text back (opacity .4, grayscale .6, scale 1.08) when a new card enters.
10. **Cutting**: remove every pause > 0.15 s; cut on speech onsets/breaths; hide jump cuts via W↔M alternation, blur-cut (20/40/20 px over 3 f), or zoom.
11. **Audio**: −14 LUFS integrated, LRA 2–4; bed −30 dBFS ducked ~15 dB; SFX −18…−24 dB. whoosh = slide/zoom/whip; pop/tick = pill/word/list; impact = brand/numeral; shimmer = logo; riser into sections.
12. **Outro**: lockup letters baseline-pop (0.2→1, 6 f, back.out 1.6, 3 f stagger) + tagline RTL typing (1 word / 3 f); or CTA; fade to black 12–36 f. (Default for talking-head videos: end on the last spoken word — craft/12.)

## What the "Before → After" delta always contains
Pauses cut · framing changes (W/M or smooth zooms) · keyword/caption typography · 1 container style (images/UI only under the soft-text default) · a depth layer · SFX + music bed · loudness normalisation · an ending that lands (last word, CTA or lockup). That is the minimum bar for every delivery. Grade + vignette only when the user wants a look.
